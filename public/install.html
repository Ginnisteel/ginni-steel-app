Add Ginni Steel to Your Home Screen

Takes 30 seconds \u2014 do this once and it stays on your phone like any other app

\ud83d\udcf1 iPhone
\ud83e\udd16 Android
1

\ud83e\udded Open in Safari

Go to ginni-steel-app.onrender.com using the Safari app (the blue compass). This only works in Safari, not Chrome, on iPhone.

2

\u2b06\ufe0f Tap the Share button

At the bottom (or top) of the screen, tap the square icon with an arrow pointing up.

3

\u2795 Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the list of options and tap it \u2014 it has a small "+" icon next to it.

4

\u2705 Tap "Add"

Confirm in the top-right corner. The Ginni Steel icon now appears on your home screen.

1

\ud83c\udf10 Open in Chrome

Go to ginni-steel-app.onrender.com using the Chrome app.

2

\u22ee Tap the menu (\u22ee)

Top-right corner of the screen \u2014 three dots stacked vertically.

3

\ud83d\udcf2 Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

Whichever wording your Chrome shows \u2014 both do the same thing.

4

\u2705 Tap "Install" or "Add"

The Ginni Steel icon now appears on your home screen, ready to use.

\ud83d\udca1 Once added, just tap the Ginni Steel icon any time \u2014 it opens straight to your order screen, full-screen, without any browser bar.
